# Surface brief — index.html (portofolio)

## Scope and mode

Single-page portfolio, **Experience** mode, overhaul: the civil-works site replaces
the ruled-sheet ledger. The artifact (a monitored construction site) leads from the
first viewport; the interface is raw, ledgered, and unframed.

## Audience, job, action

- Dev recruiters evaluating a junior front-end developer in minutes.
- Indonesian small-business/design clients deciding whether to commission.
- Action: reach KONTAK (WhatsApp/email) via the contact register in section 05 or the
  toolbar route.

## Proof and content

- Proof by construction: ledge beams, hazard-splice rails, concrete panels, bilingual
  register cells, and the touch-shift states are real, working craft — no framework.
- Karya tiles are schematic study plates, stamped `CONTOH / SAMPLE` and footnoted as
  samples in both languages, never presented as client work. No fabricated projects or
  testimonials.
- Real facts only: name, roles, skills, contact channels, photo.
- Copy is bilingual: Bahasa Indonesia is the spoken voice; English is the annotation
  voice on every structured label.
- Hard border/focus/contrast armature demonstrates accessibility practice.

## Chosen direction

**RULLY PANJI M.P. — Civil Works Site** (overhaul, dials VARIANCE 7 / MOTION 4 /
DENSITY 5): concrete-grey neutrals + safety-orange single accent, sharp 0px corners,
2px ink rulings, a ledge beam opening every section (solid ref slab 01-05 + mono name +
diagonal hazard splice), register-cell data rows, grayscale framed portrait on a
concrete panel, work-order form. Memorable moment: the ledge grammar itself — every
section a structure in progress, read as a monitored site file.

## Constraints

- Plain HTML/CSS/JS, zero build, mobile-first (Indonesian audience).
- Light default with GELAP/TERANG toggle persisted to localStorage.
- Bilingual labels: structured copy written ID + EN together; prose stays Indonesian.
- Hazard splices limited to ledge rails, the footer rail, and the hero plate clip.
- Reduced-motion path collapses reveals/pops to static; keyboard operable; WCAG AA.
- Anti-patterns: rounded corners, drop shadows, gradients, em-dashes, decorative
  eyebrows, infinite motion, framed fake-screenshot art.

## Unresolved

- Real project case studies (user replaces the sample tiles).
- Portrait source quality of `foto-profil.jpg` (rendered grayscale 4:4.6 in a ruled
  plate on a concrete panel).
- Hostable OG thumbnail; current og:image points at the local photo file.
