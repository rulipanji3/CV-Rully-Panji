## Product Principles

1. **Dual-track honesty.** Represent development and design with equal weight; neither
   track should read as decoration for the other.
2. **Evidence over assertion.** Never fabricate projects, clients, testimonials, or
   experience claims. Placeholder work is labeled as such or omitted.
3. **Proof by construction.** The portfolio must itself demonstrate front-end craft —
   it is the strongest evidence a dev recruiters sees.
4. **Decision in seconds.** A visitor should grasp the dual value prop and reach a
   contact channel within one viewport.
5. **Mobile-first.** The Indonesian audience skews mobile; the experience must be
   excellent at phone widths before desktop is considered.
